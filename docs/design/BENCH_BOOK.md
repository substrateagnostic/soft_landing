# THE BENCH BOOK — twelve ready-to-fire judged lanes for THE BIG NAP

*Authored 2026-08-18 by the ILL WILL director at the producer's request. Each entry
is a fireable bench: chairs, evidence contract, bars. Pair with CLAUDE.md §1–§3
(the loop, the precedent clause, instrument honesty). Every judge brief opens with
the SHARED PREAMBLE below. Chairs: one Opus 5 xhigh + one Fable high; implementors
Opus xhigh / gpt-5.6-sol per AGENTS.md routing. Loop: build → two chairs → full fix
(S+M) → re-judge → court-dress (S only), 3-round cap, director tiebreak.*

## SHARED PREAMBLE (paste into every judge brief)
> You are judging THE BIG NAP — a 3D collectathon platformer for a 4-year-old
> (Ezra) and their parent, two pads on one couch. Comps: Mario 64 / Odyssey's
> movement joy, Kirby's forgiveness, Banjo's place-making — judge whether this
> EXCEEDS that bar *for this player*. The design floor (AGENTS.md) is law, not
> billable. You receive the settled-rulings register (docs/DECISIONS.md), frozen
> VERIFY records, and the banked ledger: do not re-litigate ruled law or re-bill
> banked items; bill new defects freely; to reopen a ruling you need a cosign
> from your co-judge or the director. Verdict EXCEEDS/MEETS/BELOW + an itemized
> S/M/L bill, each item tied to a named clip/still/receipt. Measured claims beat
> impressions. Temporal evidence outranks stills — a still cannot prove feel.
> Nothing outranks the couch: producer-reported observed play is a ruling.

**B1 · THE FEEL BENCH** — Game-Feel Director + Child-Input Director.
Evidence: `--write-movie` clips of scripted input sequences (jump arcs, edge
walks, turn-arounds), input-to-motion latency receipt (frame-counted), the
tuning resource dump, coyote/buffer coverage sweep (harness fires jump N ms
after ledge, asserts forgiveness window honored). Bars: latency ≤3 frames at
60fps; 100% of forgiveness-window presses succeed; apex hang visible in the
velocity trace; one-stick+jump sufficiency grepped. The flagship bench — sit it
after ANY movement/physics change.

**B2 · THE CAMERA BENCH** — Camera Director + Child-Readability Director.
Evidence: motion clips through every world's worst geometry (tight interiors,
gap crossings, the mountain ascent), camera velocity-continuity trace (no spike
> bar on cuts/recoveries), occlusion-recovery clip set, the no-right-stick grep
receipt. Bars: zero hard cuts in free roam; recovery ≤ N seconds without input;
pitch biases down over every gap (sweep); subject never lost >0.5s in the clip
set. D4/D18 are settled law — carry the register.

**B3 · THE RESCUE BENCH (the Soft Landing itself)** — Game-Feel + Child-Emotion
chair (does the rescue feel like being caught, not corrected?). Evidence: fall-
path coverage sweep (harness walks every ledge/void, asserts a rescue fires —
the vacuous-pass rule applies: the sweep prints its denominator), rescue clip
reel, return-placement audit (never placed somewhere scarier). Bars: 100% of
falls rescued; bubble ride length inside its ruled window; zero rescues into
softlock; the reel READ by both chairs' actual eyes.

**B4 · THE WORLD BENCH** (per world, the doc-65 analog) — Environment Art
Director + Staging/Composition Director. Before the lane fires, the director
writes the world's **tenderness sentence** (its REASON — the bell-crypt lesson).
Evidence: still battery at the true gameplay camera + hero framings, no-void
sweep at every azimuth, giant's-body landmark readability at child eye-height,
blankstill gate, solo perf pair. Bars: place/story read without words; landmark
visible from every objective; palette inside ART_BIBLE; 60fps floor held.

**B5 · THE READABILITY BENCH** — Child-Readability Director + UX Director.
Evidence: every icon at shipped size, silhouette-only render pass, TTS script +
timing audit, colorblind triple-render, "what do I do next" walkthrough (a
fresh agent plays each objective from a cold save and logs every moment of
confusion). Bars: zero required reading (grep + eyes); every objective
discoverable from its world alone; TTS covers every must-understand beat.

**B6 · THE COUCH CO-OP BENCH** — Co-op Design Director + Game-Feel Director.
Evidence: two-pad session clips (join, drop, rejoin mid-air), split/merge
camera behavior reel (D27 v3 is settled — register), buddy-AI solo reel (does
Otto read as a friend, not a script?), asymmetry audit (Pip-small vs Otto-big
verbs both matter). Bars: join-to-playing ≤5s; zero states where one player's
camera abandons them; buddy-AI never blocks, never steals a collectible.

**B7 · THE LULLABY BENCH (audio)** — Audio Director + Game-Feel Director.
Evidence: LUFS/dB traces per world (the −21.6dB WAKING receipt is the house
precedent), ducking-behavior clips (WHISPER SPOT: music ducks to silence),
variant coverage of repeated SFX, TTS voice warmth review by ear. Bars: no
startle transients above ruled ceiling; silence beats actually silent; every
repeated verb has ≥3 variants; both chairs LISTEN, not just read traces.

**B8 · THE MEMORY BENCH (progress/persistence)** — Systems Director + Child-
Emotion chair. Evidence: cross-session persistence sweep (collect → quit →
relaunch → world remembers, ×3 cycles), hub-population reel (returned
dreamlings visibly HOME — the fantasy's payoff), memory-album audit, no-skill-
gate sweep of every unlock edge. Bars: zero progress loss; every returned
dreamling findable in the hub; save/quit safe at ANY moment including mid-air.

**B9 · THE COUCH-BOX PERF BENCH** — Tech Director + Camera Director.
Evidence: solo perf pairs (the perf117 methodology: solo-stamped, both arms,
draws/prims printed) on the couch machine, worst-world worst-camera sweep,
memory growth over a 30-minute harness session. Bars: 60fps floor at shipped
settings on the couch box (define once, freeze as this repo's watchline — do
NOT import ILL WILL's 16.6ms); zero hitches >2 frames in the sweep.

**B10 · THE FIRST-FIVE-MINUTES BENCH (onboarding)** — Child-Readability +
Game-Feel. Evidence: a cold-boot reel from power-on to first dreamling returned
with zero adult help modeled (harness drives only floor-level inputs), title
screen audit (one button starts), attract/idle behavior. Bars: playing within
60s of boot; first success within 3 minutes; nothing on screen a pre-reader
must decode to proceed.

**B11 · THE JOY-PASS BENCH (delight per beat)** — Creative Director + Child-
Emotion chair. For beat lists like the bear path (heartbeat crossing, whisper
spot, snore-geyser…): evidence is one clip per beat + a repeatability note
(does it delight the 5th time?). Bars per beat: telegraphed (no surprise-
scares), safe (floor holds), repeatable, and it earns its tenderness sentence.
Judges may kill a beat — delight that reads as filler bills L, "cut."

**B12 · THE GATE BENCH (per ROADMAP gate / per milestone exit, e.g. D25's
mountain)** — the two chairs the milestone most needs + the director. Evidence:
the milestone's own DoD checklist receipted line by line (UNVERIFIED stays
honest), a full-playthrough movie of the new content, the settled-register
delta (what this milestone rules for the future). Bars: every DoD line green
or explicitly UNVERIFIED with a reason; the couch question written for Alex —
one specific thing to watch Ezra do, stated before the playtest, so the couch
verdict lands on evidence.
