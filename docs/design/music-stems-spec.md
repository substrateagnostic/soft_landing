# Viola Stem Spec — THE BIG NAP
*For the producer. Record whenever, wherever; no access to the build machine
needed. Placeholder synth stems ship until these arrive, so nothing blocks.*

## The idea
Each giant sleeps to its own lullaby. The lullaby is built from **stems** —
independent layers that all start together and loop together. When a dream
comes home, one more layer fades in. Ten dreams = the whole lullaby. Your
viola IS the giant's dream-life becoming audible.

## Technical contract (per world)
- **Form:** one clean loop, **32 seconds** at **60 BPM in 4/4** (= 8 bars).
  Every stem exactly the same length, starting at bar 1 beat 1 (record to a
  click or to the reference synth stem; I'll trim/align if you leave lead-in).
- **Delivery format:** WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono or stereo — whatever your
  interface gives. Name files `bramble_layer_2.wav` etc. Drop them anywhere
  on D:\ (or a phone recording in a pinch — I'll clean it).
- **Loop-friendly writing:** end each phrase so bar 8 resolves INTO bar 1
  (the loop point should feel like a breath, not a cut). Sustained final
  notes are fine — I'll crossfade the tail.
- **Reference stems** to play against: `assets/audio/stems/bramble/layer_*.ogg`
  (synth placeholders, same key/tempo/length — they're in the repo once the
  push lands, or I'll send them to your phone on request).

## Bramble the bear (world 1) — D major, lullaby register
| Layer | Role | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (plays from first visit) | The breath | Long open drones, D and A, swelling roughly with a 5-second breath cycle (the chest onscreen breathes at 5 s — you're playing the bear). Con sord. if you have a mute. |
| 2 (unlocks ~1 dream) | The lullaby line | The main melodic cell — simple, singable, the thing Ezra might hum later. Think "hushabye" contour: rise a little, fall to rest. Mid register. |
| 3 (~5 dreams) | The dots | Sparse pizzicato on offbeats — fireflies. More silence than notes. |
| 4 (10 dreams — complete) | The shimmer | Harmonics, high and barely-there — starlight over the meadow. |

## Register guidance (the whole game)
Hushed and huge. No urgency anywhere, ever. Vibrato warm and slow. If a
phrase could wake a sleeping child, make it softer. Silence is a member of
the ensemble. Mistakes that sound human are better than takes that sound
careful — this is a parent humming, not a concert.
