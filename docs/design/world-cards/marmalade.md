# World Card — MARMALADE the cat (world 2)

**One line:** an enormous marmalade cat asleep across a tiny hillside
village, dreaming of warm windowsills.

**Register:** the warmest world — lamplit, terracotta, cozy. The village is
asleep too (no people — it's late); every window glows honey. The cat's purr
is a felt rumble that swells when dreams come home.

**Palette:** marmalade orange `#D98E4A` (cat), terracotta `#B4654A` (roofs),
cream plaster `#EFE6D8` (walls), honey `#F2C879` (windows, lanterns), dusk
sage `#7C9082` (the hill). Dreamlings `#FFF3C4`.

**The body as playground (movement gifts):**
- **Rooftop hop chain**: the classic — a winding chain of flat-topped
  terracotta roofs (gaps 1.2-2.0 m, all coyote-friendly), rising up the hill
  toward the cat.
- **The tail bridge**: Marmalade's tail sweeps slowly (±25° yaw around its
  base, 8 s cycle, AnimatableBody) between two rooftop clusters — a moving
  bridge you ride or time. THE signature gift of this world.
- **Purr thermals**: two chimneys puff warm updrafts on the purr cycle
  (geyser pattern, 6 m lift, cozy smoke-wisp visual).
- **The flank steps**: the cat's folded paws form big soft steps up the flank
  — the no-timing route to the summit (every gift has a patient alternative).
- **Ear peaks**: the twin ears are the summit; between them, the DreamDoor.

**Layout:** village square spawn (hill base, −X) → lane past houses →
rooftop chain → tail-bridge crossing OR flank steps → back ridge → head →
between-the-ears door. Cat ~70 m nose-to-tail draped over the hill crest.

**Dreamlings (10, D12 cadence, ids d01-d10):** d01 village square, d02 on a
low awning (first jump), d03 mid rooftop chain, d04 on a chimney top, d05 on
the paw steps (open) — d06 rides the tail tip (parented to the tail), d07
atop a purr thermal (ride it), d08 on the high ridge shelf reachable by
tail-bridge OR flank-steps route (never one-route-only), d09 hidden behind a
rooftop garden's tall pots (glow visible), d10 beside the ear DreamDoor.

**DreamDoor:** between the ears; trigger ≥3.6 wide per the bramble lesson
(covers standing above AND beside — never edge-only).

**Camera hints (≥3):** village approach (frame cat-over-village silhouette),
rooftop run (tighter, yaw along the chain), summit (frame door + the whole
village glowing below — the reward view).

**Rescue floor:** below the village foundations (-8). Streets are safe
ground everywhere — falling off a roof lands you in a lane, never a pit;
the void ring beyond the hill sits below the rescue line (bramble-moat
pattern: any true walk-off = Soft Landing).

**Audio hooks:** world stem set `stems/marmalade/` (placeholder synth:
G major, pizzicato-forward; layer_1 is the purr — a soft filtered low
rumble). SFX reuse: snore_geyser for thermals until dedicated.

**No directional light of its own** (main owns the moon); window/lantern
OmniLights are the world's warmth, kept low-energy.

**Fort growth:** dreams returned here count toward the same total (D14).
