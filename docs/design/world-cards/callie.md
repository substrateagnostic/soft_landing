# Character Card — CALLIE the sidekick
*Based on Ezra's real favorite stuffy (Ty beanie calico, "Chip"). Producer
request 2026-07-16; reference screenshot supplied. This one is personal —
build her with care.*

**What she is:** the kids' stuffed cat, brought along on the adventure. NOT
a giant (Marmalade holds that seat) — Callie is stuffy-sized (0.35 m nose to
tail), floppy, beloved.

**Look (from the reference, binding):** lying-down beanbag calico cat — warm
tan/brown head with a BLACK patch over one side of the face, black back and
body, white chest/belly/paws, white-tipped tail, small triangular ears (tan
outer, white inner), amber eyes, pink nose. Floppy understuffed pose —
she drapes, never stands.

**Behavior:**
- **Home:** naps on a fort cushion. Slow breathing bob (everything alive in
  this game breathes; Callie counts).
- **Pick up:** interact within 1.2 m → hops to the carrier (perches near the
  head/shoulder — visually distinct from the dreamling orbit below). Either
  kid can carry her. Interact again → set down where you stand (she flops,
  then settles into a nap loop).
- **Dream-sniffing (her gift):** while carried, if an UNCOLLECTED dreamling
  is within ~8 m, she mews softly (cooldown ≥4 s) and leans/looks toward it.
  A diegetic hint system for the hidden-category dreams (d09s) — no arrows,
  no text, no requirement (the floor stays: everything findable without her).
- **Purr:** soft loop while her carrier has dreamlings in orbit.
- **Going home:** on world switch she stays with you; if set down and left
  behind in another world, she's back on her fort cushion next time you're
  home (stuffies teleport; every parent knows).
- **The one Moon line** (once per save, in marmalade world while carrying
  her): "Callie dreams of being big someday." Never explained further.

**Future seams (note, don't build):** Callie as a possible third simplified
seat for Ezra's little brother someday (single-button "Callie mode" — the
Garden Train tradition); Callie reacting to Bramble's snores (ear twitch).

**SFX:** mew_soft (tiny, breathy, never insistent), purr_loop (low, warm,
loopable ~4 s). Same register laws as everything: pleasant at low volume.

**Meshy prompt (id "callie", target_height_hint 0.18 — she's LYING down):**
"beanie baby style stuffed calico cat toy lying down flat and floppy,
understuffed beanbag plush, warm tan brown head with black patch over one
side of face, black back and body, white chest belly and paws, white tipped
tail, small triangular ears, tiny amber eyes, pink nose, cute sleepy face"
+ house suffix.
